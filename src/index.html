Whisker Witchery

Indulge in the world of witchcraft and tiny thievery! In this 3D stealth platformer, Whisker Witchery follows the journey of a cursed rat exploring a magical world. After their rival witch transformed them into a rat, you must sneak through the rival witch's cottage and collect potion ingredients to become human again. Trek through the spellbound levels influenced by potions, spells, and magic while evading deathly falls and fire-spitting enemies!

Will you survive the dangers and reach the witch's cauldron? Take your chances in the stealthy adventure of Whisker Witchery!

Leaf it Behind

Restore peace to the storm-wrecked forest! In this 2D sidescroller fishing platformer, play as a flying leopard gecko with a trusty fishing rod. The locals lost their precious belongings in a terrible storm, and Little Bitty is determined to glide across the forest and fish them back up! Meet a cozy cast of animal characters, glide through this serene forest map, and experience one of the most dramatic fishing mini-games in this 36-hour game jam creation.

HiveQuest

Bee-come the hero in this bug-sized adventure! In a far-off woodland, someone steals the Honeybee Kingdom's entire supply of honey overnight! Amidst the hive's panic, set off on an adventure as Dot, a newly-promoted worker bee, and take down the honey-thieving culprit in this 2.5D RPG adventure experience.

Brawl against the prime suspects, socialize with other kingdoms at The Buzz, and triumph levels brimming with enemies, collectibles, and silly interludes. Strategically use your honey-themed skills and items to find the honey thief and make your kingdom proud!

Senior Hearts

Rescue the hearts of these old soul pups! Welcome to Senior Hearts, an empathetic story-driven game that introduces the lesser-known struggles of old dogs in the shelter system. The Senior Hearts Sanctuary saves older dogs from euthanasia in shelters and gives them a second chance at finding their fur-ever home. Play as a designated dog therapist, Dr. Bones, the day after one of the dogs pass away from heart disease.

Earn the camaraderie of this game's old soul cast and explore the four different adoption endings!
