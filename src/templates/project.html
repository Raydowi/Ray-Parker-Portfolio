In this 2D sidescroller fishing platformer, play as a flying leopard gecko with a trusty fishing rod. The locals lost their precious belongings in a terrible storm, and Little Bitty is determined to glide across the forest and fish them back up! Meet a cozy cast of animal characters, glide through this serene forest map, and experience one of the most dramatic fishing mini-games in this 36-hour game jam creation.
