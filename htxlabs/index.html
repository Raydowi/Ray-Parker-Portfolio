EMPACT is an XR platform that provides immersive training content for the U.S. Department of Defense and U.S. Air Force. Through HTX Labs, EMPACT delivers almost a decade of XR mission-readiness content and solutions for VR, web, and mobile platforms. As a gameplay engineer from 2023 thru today, I designed, engineered, and collaborated on dozens of published training simulations for EMPACT's catalog. Besides a few public EMPACT procedures, the rest of my documentation, code, and overall work are private under HTX Labs
